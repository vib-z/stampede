# Hero storyboard

One ask, a coordinated team, one reply. A phone sends a request to build a pricing page. The lead opens its team; each agent's work changes the page. Checked work returns to the lead, and the finished page remains beside the reply.

## Sequence

Each cut contains 420 poses at 30 fps. The film is silent and can be scrubbed in either direction.

| Frames | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 0-53 | The ask types at 50 ms per character and sends once. On portrait, the phone starts receding at frame 52. |
| 54-83 | A larger local lead receives the ask. Workers emerge from behind it before it moves aside. |
| 84-128 | Research papers gather and three notes are pinned. |
| 129-191 | Two builders cross the connection. Their location badges enlarge, hold for 12 frames and morph from local to cloud at Tailscale. |
| 192-257 | Paper tabs open and three pricing cards assemble. Design guides align the cards and the title arrives. |
| 258-317 | A pointer tests the button. A bug emerges and is squashed. A magnifier examines the page; the reviewer stamps it twice. |
| 318-389 | Builders return checked slips, the lead gathers the work, and the reply returns to the phone after the page settles. |
| 390-419 | The lead closes over ten frames. The reply and completed page hold for the final 20 frames. |

## Composition

Desktop is 1920x1080, with pricing cards in columns and the finished page beside the phone. Portrait is 1080x1920, with cards in rows and the finished page below the phone. Both use the same beat boundaries and preserve space for the page title and actions.

Every agent carries a small location badge. Lead, research, test and review stay local. Build and design change from local to cloud during their outbound crossing and remain cloud on their return. Tailscale's mark appears at the crossing; checked work follows the connection home.

The scene uses white or near-black ground and neutral grey materials. Paper has layered cut edges, grain, contact shadows and a raking highlight. A soft light pool follows the work. The generic phone has a separate rim, screen and shadow. Official agent marks retain their supplied colours; the surrounding page adds gold Star actions.

The Open gate mark plays once above the title. The title, tagline and actions are visible immediately. Only "Your call." and "All agents closed." appear as film captions. The final phone-and-page composition also supplies the reduced-motion and no-JavaScript poster.
